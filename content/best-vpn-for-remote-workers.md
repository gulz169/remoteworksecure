## The short answer

If you work from home, cafes or co-working spaces, a VPN encrypts your connection on networks you do not control. For most remote workers, NordVPN or Surfshark offer the best balance of price and features, based on their published plans. ExpressVPN is simpler but costs more. Proton VPN has a useful free tier. Mullvad is the most privacy-focused option.

This article is based on published information, not a hands-on review. We have not tested these products ourselves.

## NordVPN

NordVPN is a good default choice for remote work. It supports WireGuard through NordLynx, has servers in the UK and US, and includes a kill switch. Its affiliate programme pays 40% on 1- and 2-year plans, plus 30% on renewals.

Pros: fast protocol, large server network, kill switch.
Cons: long-term plans require upfront payment.

## Surfshark

Surfshark allows unlimited simultaneous devices, which is useful if you work on a laptop, phone and tablet. It also supports WireGuard and includes a kill switch.

Pros: unlimited devices, lower long-term price.
Cons: fewer advanced features than NordVPN for some users.

## ExpressVPN

ExpressVPN is the easiest to set up. Its Lightway protocol is designed for speed and reliability on public Wi-Fi. It costs more than NordVPN or Surfshark.

Pros: very easy to use, reliable on public Wi-Fi.
Cons: higher price, no unlimited devices.

## Proton VPN

Proton VPN offers a free tier with no data limit, which is rare. Paid plans add more servers, faster speeds and streaming support.

Pros: free tier, strong privacy reputation.
Cons: free tier has fewer servers and slower speeds.

## Mullvad

Mullvad charges a flat monthly price and does not require an email address to sign up. It is the best choice if privacy matters more than streaming or extra features.

Pros: flat price, no email required, strong privacy.
Cons: fewer servers, no long-term discount.

## Which VPN should you buy?

- Choose NordVPN if you want a safe default for remote work.
- Choose Surfshark if you have many devices or a small team.
- Choose ExpressVPN if you want the simplest setup.
- Choose Proton VPN if you want to start free.
- Choose Mullvad if privacy is your top priority.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is public Wi-Fi safe for work?

Public Wi-Fi is riskier than a home network because you do not control it. A VPN encrypts your traffic, but you should also use two-factor authentication and avoid logging in to sensitive accounts on networks you do not trust.

### Do I need a VPN if I work from home?

A VPN is less important on a trusted home network, but it still hides your traffic from your internet provider and helps when you travel or work from cafes.

### Can I use a free VPN for remote work?

A free VPN can work for light use, but free plans often limit speed, servers and data. For daily remote work, a paid plan with a money-back guarantee is usually better.
