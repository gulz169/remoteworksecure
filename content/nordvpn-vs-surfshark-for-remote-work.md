## The short answer

For most remote workers, NordVPN is the safer default. It has a larger server network, more advanced security features and a long track record. Surfshark is the better choice if you work on many devices, share a subscription with a small team or family, or want a lower long-term price. Both are good VPNs. The right one depends on how many devices you use and how much control you want over your setup.

This article is based on published information, not a hands-on review. We have not tested these products ourselves.

## NordVPN

NordVPN is one of the largest VPN providers. It offers servers in many countries, including the UK and the US, and supports WireGuard through its NordLynx protocol. A kill switch blocks your internet connection if the VPN drops, so your real IP address is not exposed by accident. NordVPN also offers extra features such as a dedicated IP address, double VPN servers and threat protection, which blocks some trackers and malicious websites.

For remote work, the most useful part is reliability. If you work from cafes, co-working spaces or hotels, you need a VPN that connects quickly and stays connected. NordVPN has apps for Windows, Mac, iOS, Android and Linux, and browser extensions for Chrome, Firefox and Edge.

Pros: large server network, NordLynx protocol, kill switch, extra security features, apps for every device.
Cons: long-term plans require upfront payment, only 10 simultaneous devices, dedicated IP costs extra.

## Surfshark

Surfshark is the main rival to NordVPN for remote workers, and its biggest advantage is simple: unlimited simultaneous devices. You can protect a laptop, a phone, a tablet and a work computer on one subscription, and share it with a partner or a small team. Surfshark also supports WireGuard, has a kill switch and split tunnelling, and offers a CleanWeb feature that blocks some ads and trackers.

Surfshark is usually cheaper than NordVPN on long-term plans, based on published prices. The trade-off is a smaller server network and fewer advanced features. For most remote workers, that does not matter. If you mainly need a VPN for public Wi-Fi, for working from abroad, or for keeping your traffic private from your internet provider, Surfshark covers all of it.

Pros: unlimited devices, lower long-term price, kill switch, CleanWeb, easy to use.
Cons: smaller server network than NordVPN, fewer advanced features, dedicated IP costs extra.

## Which one is better for public Wi-Fi?

Both are good on public Wi-Fi. The important features are the same: a kill switch, modern protocols and apps that connect quickly. If you work from a cafe once a week, either one will do the job. If you work from public networks every day, NordVPN has a small edge because of its larger network and extra security tools, but Surfshark is not far behind.

## Which one is better for a small team?

Surfshark wins for a small team. Unlimited devices means one subscription can cover the whole team, instead of buying a separate plan for each person. NordVPN limits you to 10 devices, which is enough for one person but not for a team sharing an account. If your team needs admin controls and separate accounts, look at a business VPN plan instead of sharing a personal subscription.

## Which one is better for working abroad?

Both work for working abroad, but check two things before you travel. First, check that the VPN has servers in the country you need. Second, check that the VPN works in that country, because some countries restrict VPN traffic. NordVPN has servers in more countries, which gives you more options. Surfshark covers the most common destinations, including the UK, the US and most of Europe.

## How to choose

- Choose NordVPN if you want a safe default, a larger network and more security features.
- Choose Surfshark if you have many devices, share with a partner or team, or want a lower price.
- Choose either one if you only need a VPN for public Wi-Fi and basic privacy, because both cover that well.

Do not choose based on a single discount. Long-term plans are cheaper per month, but you pay upfront for one or two years. Check the renewal price as well, because it is often higher than the first-term price.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a VPN if I work from home?

A VPN is less important on a trusted home network, but it still hides your traffic from your internet provider and helps when you travel or work from cafes. If you only work from home and never use public Wi-Fi, a VPN is optional.

### Can I use one subscription on my work laptop and my phone?

Yes, with both NordVPN and Surfshark. NordVPN allows 10 devices at the same time. Surfshark allows unlimited devices, so you can protect every device you own.

### Will a VPN slow down my internet?

A VPN can slow your connection a little, because your traffic is encrypted and routed through a VPN server. The slowdown is usually small on a fast connection. If your internet is already slow, try a server closer to your location, or switch protocols in the app settings.

### Is it safe to use a free VPN instead?

A free VPN can work for light use, but free plans often limit speed, servers and data. Some free VPNs also make money from advertising or from selling data. For daily remote work, a paid plan with a 30-day money-back guarantee is usually a better choice.
