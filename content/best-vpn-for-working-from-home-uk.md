## What matters for working from home in the UK

### UK servers

If you live in the UK, you need a VPN with servers in the UK. That sounds obvious, but it matters for two reasons. First, a UK server gives you the fastest connection, because your traffic does not travel far. Second, some UK services, such as BBC iPlayer and UK banking apps, work best when your connection appears to be in the UK. All of the VPNs in this article have UK servers, based on their published server lists.

### Speed

Working from home means video calls, large file uploads and cloud tools. A VPN can slow your connection a little, because your traffic is encrypted and routed through a VPN server. On a fast UK broadband connection, the slowdown is usually small. If your connection is already slow, choose a server in London or Manchester, which are close to most UK homes, and choose a modern protocol such as WireGuard in the app settings.

### Devices

Count your devices before you choose. A typical remote worker has a laptop, a phone and a tablet, plus a work laptop. That is four devices. NordVPN allows 10, ExpressVPN allows 8, and Surfshark allows unlimited. If you share with a partner or a family, Surfshark is the easiest option, because you do not need to count devices at all.

### Price

Long-term plans are cheaper per month than monthly plans, but you pay upfront for one or two years. Check the renewal price as well as the first-term price, because the renewal price is often higher.

## NordVPN

NordVPN is a good default choice for working from home in the UK. It has servers in multiple UK locations, supports WireGuard through its NordLynx protocol, and includes a kill switch. It also offers extra features such as a dedicated IP address and threat protection, which blocks some trackers and malicious websites. If you want a VPN that just works and has plenty of options, NordVPN is a safe pick.

Pros: large server network, UK servers in several cities, NordLynx protocol, kill switch.
Cons: long-term plans require upfront payment, only 10 simultaneous devices.

## Surfshark

Surfshark allows unlimited simultaneous devices, which is useful if you work on a laptop, a phone and a tablet, and share with a partner. It also supports WireGuard, includes a kill switch, and offers a CleanWeb feature that blocks some ads and trackers. Surfshark is usually cheaper than NordVPN on long-term plans, based on published prices.

Pros: unlimited devices, lower long-term price, CleanWeb, easy to use.
Cons: fewer advanced features than NordVPN for some users, smaller server network.

## ExpressVPN

ExpressVPN is the easiest to set up. Its Lightway protocol is designed for speed and reliability, and its apps are simple to use. It costs more than NordVPN or Surfshark, but if you want the least hassle and you are happy to pay for it, ExpressVPN is a good choice.

Pros: very easy to use, reliable on UK broadband, Lightway protocol.
Cons: higher price, no unlimited devices, fewer UK server locations than NordVPN.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a VPN if I work from home in the UK?

A VPN is less important on a trusted home network, but it still hides your traffic from your internet provider and helps when you travel or work from cafes. If you only work from home and never use public Wi-Fi, a VPN is optional.

### Will a VPN slow down my UK broadband?

A VPN can slow your connection a little, but on a fast connection the slowdown is usually small. Choose a UK server close to you, such as London or Manchester, and use a modern protocol such as WireGuard to keep the speed up.

### Is it legal to use a VPN in the UK?

Yes, using a VPN in the UK is legal. A VPN is a privacy tool. It does not make illegal activity legal, and you should still follow the law and the rules of any service you use.
